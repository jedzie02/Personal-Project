# 11:00 PM Operating Close Limit Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Cap the venue's operating hours at 11:00 PM (`23:00`), removing the midnight slot and ensuring the final 1-hour session of the night runs from 10:00 PM to 11:00 PM across customer booking and admin management.

**Architecture:** Update the closing time constant in `booking-grid.service.ts` and `court-jump.ts` from `"00:00"` to `"23:00"`. Remove `"00:00"` from `HOUR_OPTIONS` and update fallback close times in `ScheduleTimeline.tsx`. Align product documentation.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 15, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Vitest.

**Spec:** [Design Specification: 11:00 PM Operating Close Limit](#)

## Global Constraints
- Operating hours start at 9:00 AM (`"09:00"`) and close at 11:00 PM (`"23:00"`).
- Final 1-hour playable court session is 10:00 PM to 11:00 PM (`"22:00"` to `"23:00"`).
- Zero TypeScript errors (`npx tsc --noEmit`).
- All 24 Vitest unit tests pass (`npm run test`).

## Review Focus
- Booking grid hours list stops at `"22:00"` (which maps to 10:00 PM – 11:00 PM).
- No midnight hour (`"00:00"`) selectable in Admin Operating Hours drawer.
- Court jump sequence calculations do not resolve continuous slots past 11:00 PM.
